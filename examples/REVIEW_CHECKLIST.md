# Human release review sheet

- [ ] The story is original; no copied/reworded internet narrative or recognizable protected characters.
- [ ] All claims, appearances, voices, relationships and props match the series bible.
- [ ] The episode pays off its central promise and has a believable emotional consequence.
- [ ] The final reveal follows from what the viewer has been shown (not random deus ex machina).
- [ ] Character images stay recognizable across shots; hands/faces/costumes checked.
- [ ] Voices sound like the same people in each scene; text and audio agree.
- [ ] Final captions are word-aligned, readable and checked by a human (not the draft scene SRT).
- [ ] Music, SFX, models, voice and source images have commercial rights.
- [ ] Language is PG: no vulgarity, explicit sexual material, graphic violence or hate.
- [ ] Final MP4 plays with correct resolution, orientation and audio levels.
- [ ] TikTok/YouTube altered/synthetic disclosures and upload terms checked.
- [ ] Per-episode provider spend has been reviewed.
- [ ] User has approved publication manually; only then should approved canon be recorded.
