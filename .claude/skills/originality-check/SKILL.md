# Originality & Rights Audit

1. Read the candidate shotlist (or legacy script) and the titles, loglines and dialogue of earlier episodes and shows; run `check`.
2. Compare character configuration, inciting incident, causal steps, reveal and ending with our previous episodes and series.
3. Ask whether it is a thin adaptation of a known movie, short, post or viral trend. If so, rewrite causality, setting, motivation and outcome.
4. Don't search the web for stories to borrow.
5. Assets: only original provider-generated references, motion shots and lip sync, roster voices from `bible/voices.json`, no real-person or franchise look-alikes, no readable brands. Music is AI-generated per episode or owner-licensed in `assets/music/provenance.json`; SFX only with provenance in `bible/sfx/`.
7. "Social experiment" or documentary-style stories are fiction: never present staged scenes as real events, and say so in the description.
6. Add a short originality note to `reports/editorial_review.md`. The offline similarity test covers local scripts only.
