# Originality and media rights (always applies)

- Write new fiction. Don't copy, translate, closely paraphrase or scene-for-scene adapt internet, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, TV, film, music videos or novels.
- Research may identify general audience interests (e.g. family drama about rebuilding), never language, dialogue, character configurations or plots to imitate.
- If a premise resembles a familiar work, change its causality, characters, setting, stakes, sequence, reveal and ending, not just names.
- Visual assets: only original images and clips generated by our configured providers from our own prompts. No stock, web, screenshot or reference photos of real people; no celebrity, brand or franchise look-alikes; no logos or readable brand text in prompts.
- Audio: only roster voices from the owner's ElevenLabs account. No cloned or impersonated voices, no copyrighted music. Music stays off unless a commercially licensed track is listed in `assets/music/provenance.json`.
- Record provenance (provider, model, request IDs) in the episode reports. The offline similarity checker covers only local approved scripts, not the internet.
- AI-generated imagery may not be copyrightable and AI-disclosure rules vary; the owner sets platform disclosure at upload.
