# TaleNovi family-safe story quality (always applies)

- Brand is PG / broadly family-safe: no profanity/vulgarity, explicit nudity or sex, graphic violence/gore, hateful slurs, or cruelty for shock value.
- Keep emotional intensity without exploitation: hope, wonder, loss, loyalty, trust, courage, reunion and sacrifice are allowed, but resolve with meaning.
- Beginning: establish a concrete oddity and an immediate character question. Avoid deception or a fake title/hook.
- Middle: give the protagonist a choice, let the choice cause a setback, reveal a relationship cost and build stakes.
- Ending: deliver a **meaningful answer or shift** before introducing a forward question. Don't leave every part abruptly unfinished.
- Characters have distinct desires and voices. A series needs development over time, not disposable twist narrations.
- Show rather than explain where possible; visual actions must be filmable without lots of onscreen text.
- Regularity is not a quality metric. Reject redundant plots even if quotas aren't met.
- Hard themes (controlling or toxic relationships, frustration, grief) are allowed with care: show behaviour and emotional cost, no violence or abuse on screen, never glamorize control, give the affected person agency, and move toward boundaries, support and hope.
- Follow platform synthetic-media rules; Claude reviews every final video (frames + story) before marking it complete.
