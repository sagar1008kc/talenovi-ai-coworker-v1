# TaleNovi original-story standard

## Each 60–90-second vertical episode
- 0–3s: honest, striking hook with a visual and a reason to care about the person.
- 3–15s: orient viewer in place, desire and relationship.
- 15–35s: a choice creates conflict; mystery becomes personal.
- 35–55s: emotional turn, sacrifice, discovery, or unexpected kindness.
- 55–75s: reveal/partial resolution that pays off this episode's main question.
- Final seconds: an earned unresolved question linked to the ongoing character arc, not a fake stop.
These are pacing guidelines, not a rigid repetitive template; vary the form between episodes.

## Emotional progression across a five- or six-episode season (example)
1. **Curiosity → vulnerability:** first impossible signal and an old wound.
2. **Hope → doubt:** evidence connects the signal to a loved one.
3. **Trust → betrayal:** a well-intentioned choice has a cost.
4. **Fear → courage:** someone makes an honest sacrifice.
5. **Despair → connection:** relationships evolve under pressure.
6. **Loss → meaningful resolution:** answer season mystery, preserve space for new stories.
No forced tragedies or manipulative shock; endings earn audience loyalty through character meaning.

## Season story contract
- The main character has an external objective, an internal need and a specific wound.
- Supporting characters have separate motivations and relationships that change.
- Give each episode a distinct problem, set piece, emotional beat and payoff.
- Preserve the world's rules, chronology and recognizable looks/voices.
- Record approved canon in `continuity.json` only after final video review.
- Keep clips safe for broad audiences and visually intelligible even on silent playback.

## Originality review questions
1. Is the plot generated independently rather than retold from a public story?
2. Could a viewer identify a specific existing work from this scene order, character relationships, dialogue, or reveal?
3. Is this substantially different from the creator's own published episodes?
4. Do the twist and emotional arc logically emerge from our original world rules?
5. Are all assets and voices commercially usable with evidence of permission?
"Original" is an editorial goal; no software can certify worldwide uniqueness.

## v4 casting / addictive-in-a-good-way series writing
- **Earn retention instead of manipulating it.** Make the audience care about the person's choice and its consequence; avoid artificial withheld answers, false claims or perpetual unresolved cliffhangers.
- The casting director chooses 2–3 recurring leads based on the conflict, not random appearance. Each needs an external goal, psychological need, flaw, distinct voice, signature prop, and authentic relational tension.
- For each character write a *season-long internal transformation*. The earlier episodes' sacrifices and discoveries must influence future behavior. Never reset the emotional journey between episodes.
- Every episode should include a change in the relationship or knowledge state, not only one spooky object or cheap twist. Include one earned payoff and a forward question only when justified.
- The approved season outline keeps episodes varied: different locations, choices, conflict and central emotional turn while preserving the same world rules and recognizable visual and vocal identity.
- Do not optimize for shocking content, vulgarity, graphic violence, copyrighted plot theft, or engagement bait. Keep PG storytelling genuinely satisfying for viewers.
- One exported 9:16 1080×1920 MP4 (60–90 s, aim 75–85 s) is usable on both TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Motion comes from Wan image-to-video on selected shots; there is no reference-conditioned face consistency or lip sync, so spoken character lines are staged off camera.
